LastCup

That 4 PM coffee is still 40% in your blood at 11 PM. LastCup does the decay math against your bedtime and tells you exactly when to stop.

Find my cutoff

Set your bedtime and metabolism, log what you've already drunk, and the app projects how much caffeine will still be in your system tonight - then gives you the clock time of your last safe cup. Not vibes: exponential half-life math, the same decay curve sleep research uses.
